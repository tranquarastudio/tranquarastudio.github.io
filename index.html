Tranquara Studio

Put your phone on DND. Let the vibe take over.

Tranquara Studio creates relaxing ambience videos for sleep, focus and calm — rain, fireplaces, lo-fi and nature sounds in long, seamless loops.

What this application does

This is a private, personal publishing tool used by the Tranquara Studio channel owner. It is not a public service and has no other users.

The application connects to the owner's own YouTube channel to upload the studio's ambience videos and Shorts, together with their titles, descriptions and tags.

Why it requests Google account access

The application requests the following Google permissions, and nothing more:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the studio's own videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube — to set video metadata (title, description, tags) and thumbnails.

It does not read, collect or share any other Google user data. It only ever acts on the channel of its own owner.
