Privacy Policy

Tranquara Studio · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tranquara Studio publishing application ("the Application") accesses, uses, stores and shares information, including data obtained through Google APIs.

1. About the Application

The Application is a private, personal tool operated by the owner of the Tranquara Studio YouTube channel. It is used solely to publish the owner's own ambience videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public service and has no users other than its owner.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

The Application uses YouTube API Services. By using the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access

The Application requests only the minimum Google permissions required to publish videos:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • youtube — to set video metadata (title, description, tags) and thumbnails on the owner's own channel.

The Application does not access, collect, read or process any other Google user data, and does not access any channel other than its owner's.

4. How information is used

Information accessed through Google APIs is used exclusively to publish and manage the owner's own videos on the owner's own channel. It is never used for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to publishing.

5. Storage and retention

An OAuth refresh token is stored locally on the owner's own computer, solely to authenticate the Application. Video files and their metadata are stored locally on the owner's computer. No Google user data is stored on any external server, and no data is transmitted to any third party.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent or otherwise share any information obtained through Google APIs with third parties. Data is not transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers.

7. Revoking access

You may revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time via the Google security settings page. Revoking access immediately stops the Application from performing any further actions on your account.

8. Deletion of data

To request deletion of any locally stored token or data, remove the Application's credentials from the owner's computer and revoke access as described in section 7. Because the Application stores no data externally, deletion is immediate and complete.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: tranquarastudio@gmail.com
